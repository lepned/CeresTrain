## 2. Action head — primary novel surface

**Target extraction: correct, and empirically verified on the real v7 sample** (40 games / 4265 records, `_t91_v7_op1_sample`):

- Field is `q_after_played` (C# `EncodedTrainingPositionExtraV7.QAfterPlayedMove`, "*from the mover's perspective: -(next record's best_q); NaN at the last record*"). Measured `max |q_after_played + next.best_q| = 0.000000`, last-record NaN in 40/40 games.
- `d`-after = game-order shift of `best_d` (`v6_dataset.py:393-397`), done in `_decode_chunk` **before** the z-integrity filter and `skip_count` downsample — alignment is correct. Verified: `act_d[:3] == next.best_d[:3]`, `act_d[-1] == 0`.
- Sentinel: `played_idx[-1] = 0xFFFF` → `np.where(_apidx < 1858, _apidx, -1)` → probe shows `act_idx[-3:] = [485, 1075, -1]`.
- **Index space is correct**: `policies_indices` in `v6_dataset.py:487-490` are raw argsort indices into lc0's 1858 `probs` array (no remap), so `played_idx` shares the network's policy index space. Probe: `played_idx` lands on a **legal** policy slot 100.000% of the time.
- **Side-to-move perspective is correct and matches the existing exported-head convention**: the 4-board path trains `reverse(action_out1[played]) ≈ value_out2` (`train.py:2334-2335`, `wdl_reverse=[2,1,0]` at 1867), i.e. `action[played]` = child WDL *from the parent's mover's perspective* — exactly what `-next.best_q` gives. No inversion.
- WDL construction is simplex-valid: probed `w+d+l == 1.0` for all cases including the degenerate `q=0.9,d=0.9` (clamps to 0.5/0.5/0.0).

**Loss wiring / DDP / resume / export:**
- Participation on every branch: **yes** (probed, see fix #2).
- DDP: correct to omit from the guard (output returned from forward). `core.compute_loss` is called on the **uncompiled** module (`train.py:2056`), so inductor cannot constant-fold `0.0 * out.sum()` away.
- Batch transfer is generic (`_move_batch_to_device`, `train.py:93`); hard-replay buffer captures/injects **all** tensor keys with key-signature matching (`train.py:2266`, `2019-2021`) — new keys ride along safely.
- `recover_export.py` reconstructs the head (reads `Opt_LossActionPlayedMultiplier` from `config` inside `__init__`) and loads `strict=False` — fine.
- **Resume: broken** — `action_head.` is missing from `_AUX_HEAD_PREFIXES` → finding #1.
- **Export: not strippable** — finding #10.
- TPG back-compat: byte-identical (three `if v7x.X is not None` guards, `tpg_dataset.py:825-831`). Inert at weight 0 (head not constructed, `ceres_net.py:222`).
- v7x tuple did **not** grow another positional slot — the named contract replaced the `len()` discriminator. Good.

**Bottom line:** the 15 claimed fixes are real — 11 fully verified (several probe-confirmed), 4 partial (#1, #9, #11 incomplete in scope; #4/#7's gate too loose). The new action head's data path is the best-validated part of the commit: target field, sign convention, index space and sentinel handling all check out empirically against the C# spec and the real v7 sample. The two things I would fix before the 1B launch are **finding #1** (`action_head.` in `_AUX_HEAD_PREFIXES` — one-token change, otherwise the head cannot be switched on by resume) and **finding #4/#3** (tighten the population gate and mask on NaN `q_after_played` instead of trusting a 1% floor). Findings #2 and #6 are the recurring incompleteness pattern — both would be closed permanently by making `_AUX_HEAD_PREFIXES` and the DDP guard single sources of truth rather than parallel hand-maintained lists.
